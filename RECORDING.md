## Setup

**Slides**
- Open rendered `index.html` in Chrome
- Press `F` for full-screen RevealJS
- Do NOT press `S` (opens speaker notes window — will appear in recording)
- Keep speaker notes in a separate printed sheet or phone

**Recording**
- QuickTime → New Screen Recording → select the slide display → record
- Optional: PiP webcam in corner (keep small, never overlap code blocks)
- Export: File → Export As → 1080p → rename to `Thursday-John-Ehrlinger.mp4`

**Audio**
- Use headset or external mic — built-in picks up fan noise
- Record 30-second test clip; listen back with headphones before full run
- Close doors, silence phone, fans off

---

## Delivery

**Energy calibration (Nic Crane / R-Ladies tip)**  
Do one full dummy run with *exaggerated* enthusiasm first. The normal run immediately after will have the right amount of energy — you lose ~30% on camera vs. in person.

**Aim for 3–4 takes.** The second or third is usually the best — loose enough to sound natural, tight enough to stay in time. Record the whole thing each time; don't stop and restart mid-slide.

**Talk to one person, not the room.** Pick a specific colleague in your head and explain it to them.

**Timing target:** 18–19 min. If you're consistently over, the §2 problem slides (shared libraries, code/results) are the most cuttable in delivery.
